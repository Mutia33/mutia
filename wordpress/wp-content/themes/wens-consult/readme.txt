== Description ==

WENS Consult is a modern WordPress theme designed for consulting firms, business agencies, startups, and corporate service providers. Its clean and minimal layout helps businesses showcase their services, expertise, and projects effectively. As a child theme of WENS Haelo, it inherits powerful features and flexibility tailored for professional services. The theme includes essential sections like a Hero Banner with a call-to-action, Services Section, About Section, Projects Showcase, Testimonials, Blog, and Contact. This design allows businesses to present their offerings professionally. WENS Consult emphasizes a structured workflow—Discovery, Strategy, Design, and Development—which is ideal for consulting firms wanting to display their methodology clearly. The theme is fully responsive, SEO-friendly, and optimized for performance across all devices. With its customizable layout and business-focused features, WENS Consult is perfect for consultants, corporate agencies, marketing firms, IT consultants, financial advisors, and startups. It helps businesses build credibility, showcase services effectively, and generate leads with a modern design.

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance -> Themes and click the 'Add New' button.
2. Type in WENS Consult in the search form and press the 'Enter' key on your keyboard.
3. Click on the 'Activate' button to use your new theme right away.
4. Navigate to Appearance -> Editor in your admin panel and customize to taste.
